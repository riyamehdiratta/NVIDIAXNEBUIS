# Research and product positioning

**Research snapshot:** October 28, 2026. This is a small product-direction scan, not a full market-size study.

## User problem

- Microsoft says Microsoft 365 workers in its 2025 Work Trend Index telemetry are interrupted on average every two minutes by a meeting, email, or notification. This is platform telemetry and should be described as Microsoft's data, not as a representative global workforce survey: [Work Trend Index](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/breaking-down-infinite-workday).
- A 2025 survey of 1,372 employees found email overload associated with higher job stress and lower job and life satisfaction: [Information & Management study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S037872062400171X).

These sources substantiate a broad, recurring burden. They do not establish that every user has the same need or quantify the addressable market.

## Existing alternatives

- Google Gemini can use user-connected Google apps, including Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, Photos, and other supported connected apps: [Connected Apps help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16836988?hl=en-4).
- Microsoft Copilot Tasks offers task-specific memory and connectors for tasks such as drafting mail and managing a calendar: [Copilot Tasks](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/using-copilot-tasks).

Threadline should not claim that no personal assistants connect to user data or remember tasks. Its intended distinction is a provider-independent commitment ledger with inspectable evidence, portable source files, and a separate policy check for every local action.

## Inference from evidence

Because current assistants are adding app connections, memory, and tasks, the product surface is moving quickly. Threadline's credible differentiator is the auditable continuity loop: source → remembered commitment → reviewable skill → scoped action → receipt. Its value must be demonstrated in the product; it is not proven simply by using open-source models.
